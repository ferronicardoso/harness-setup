# Brainstorm

Use this skill to explore ideas, generate creative solutions, and expand thinking on any topic.

**What it does:**
- Generates multiple distinct ideas around a theme or problem (at least 5-7)
- For each idea: a clear title, the reasoning behind it, practical applications, and implementation considerations
- Offers diverse perspectives and angles rather than converging early on one answer

**When to use:**
- Creative problem-solving and innovation
- Generating new feature ideas
- Exploring solutions from multiple angles
- Planning and ideation sessions

**Method:**
1. Generate multiple creative and diverse ideas — balance practical and innovative, don't self-censor toward the obvious answer first.
2. For each idea, cover: clear title and summary, why it's valuable, practical applications, implementation considerations, and how it differs from existing approaches.
3. Ground the output in what's realistic to implement, without abandoning the creative angle that makes brainstorming worth doing.

**Output:** A short list of ideas (title + rationale + practical next step each), not a single recommendation — that synthesis is a separate, later step the user can ask for explicitly.
